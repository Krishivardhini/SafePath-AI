SafePath-AI

Assistive Mobility Companion for Independent Living

SafePath-AI combines deep learning object detection (YOLOv8), multi-object spatial tracking, monocular pinhole depth estimation, and open-source OpenStreetMap walking navigation into an all-in-one assistant.

Architecture Highlights

  • Local Deep Learning Vision: 80 COCO classes with real bounding boxes and track persistence.
  • Spatial Corridor Analysis: Evaluates center 34% forward walking corridor vs left & right peripherals.
  • Hands-Free Ask SafePath: Natural spoken responses in 8 languages (English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Spanish).
  • 100% Privacy Preserving: Zero third-party video storage or advertising tracking.
